# Vatican II: Origin, Purpose & Context

> [!summary] One-line
> On **25 Jan 1959** John XXIII surprised the Church by announcing a council; driven by **aggiornamento** (updating) and **ressourcement** (return to sources), it aimed to be **pastoral** — renewal, unity, dialogue — not condemnation.

## The announcement
**25 January 1959** — fewer than 100 days after his election, **John XXIII** told a group of cardinals at St Paul Outside the Walls he would summon a diocesan synod for Rome *and* an ecumenical council for the universal Church. Unexpected: many thought Vatican I's definitions of papal primacy/infallibility had made further councils **superfluous**.

## Context — the world of the 1960s
- Aftermath of **two World Wars** and the **Holocaust**.
- **Cold War**: divided Europe (Iron Curtain), militant Soviet atheism.
- **Globalization, decolonization, information age**, 1960s social revolutions.
- Theological ferment surviving the anti-Modernist crackdown: **biblical movement**, **liturgical renewal**, **ecumenical movement**, **patristic revival**.
- Clash: conservative **neo-Scholastics** ("fortress" mentality) vs the emerging **Nouvelle Théologie** (deep cultural engagement). → [[05 - Theologians Influence Map]]

## The two engines
> [!note] Aggiornamento
> "Updating / modernization." Adapting the Church to changing times.
> John XXIII, gesturing to a window: *"I expect a little fresh air from it… We must shake off the imperial dust that has accumulated on the throne of Saint Peter since Constantine."* #aggiornamento

> [!note] Ressourcement
> "Return to the sources" — Scripture and the Church Fathers, not only later rigid systems. The Council realized true aggiornamento is impossible **without** ressourcement. #ressourcement

## Goals — a *pastoral* council
No debate of heresy, no anathemas. *"Christ's Bride prefers the Balm of Mercy to the Arm of Severity."*
1. **Spiritual renewal** — refidelity to Christ's will.
2. **Unity (ecumenism)** — restoring unity among all Christians. → [[Unitatis Redintegratio]]
3. **Pastoral engagement** — dialogue with the modern world, presenting doctrine intelligibly. → [[Gaudium et Spes]]

## The opening
**11 October 1962** — Council opens with John XXIII's visionary address ***Gaudet Mater Ecclesia***, rejecting the *"prophets of gloom, who are always forecasting disaster."*
