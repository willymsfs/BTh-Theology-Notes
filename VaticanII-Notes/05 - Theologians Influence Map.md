# Which Theologian Shaped Which Document

> [!summary] One-line
> The *periti* could not vote but authored and revised the texts. Map: **Philips → Lumen Gentium**, **Murray → Dignitatis Humanae**, **Chenu → Gaudium et Spes ("signs of the times")**, **Rahner → LG 16**, **de Lubac/Congar → People of God**, **Bea → Dei Verbum**, **Ratzinger → ressourcement across texts**.

## The map
| Theologian | Document / Area | What they shaped |
|---|---|---|
| [[Gerard Philips]] | [[Lumen Gentium]] | Chief redactor after the "triumphalistic" draft was rejected; shifted focus to the **"Mystery of the Church,"** made it strongly **pneumatological** (Holy Spirit as agent of salvation). |
| [[John Courtney Murray]] | [[Dignitatis Humanae]] | Decisive drafter of **religious liberty**; earlier censured by the Vatican; defended the **religiously neutral state** and rejection of coercion in belief. |
| [[Marie-Dominique Chenu]] | [[Gaudium et Spes]] | Theology of the **"signs of the times"**; modern developments as *loci theologici in act*; use of social sciences to read the Word in history. |
| [[Karl Rahner]] | [[Lumen Gentium]] §16 | Transcendental method + **"Anonymous Christianity"**; framework for God's **universal salvific will** and openness to non-Christians. |
| [[Henri de Lubac]] | [[Lumen Gentium]] + [[Gaudium et Spes]] | Nouvelle Théologie; Church as **"Community of the Whole People of God"** not only clergy. Later **critical** of GS's optimistic anthropology. |
| [[Yves Congar]] | Ecclesiology / **collegiality** | Champion of the laity & **People of God**; noted VII's ecclesiology is more **Trinitarian** than Vatican I's; used Aquinas for **Church-as-communion** and episcopal collegiality. |
| [[Edward Schillebeeckx]] | Collegiality / secularization | Stressed the **Episcopal College**; argued the Church must redefine its stance toward a secularized world → feeds [[Gaudium et Spes]]. |
| [[Augustin Bea]] | [[Dei Verbum]] | Head of Secretariat for Christian Unity; co-led (with Ottaviani) the **mixed commission** that reworked the revelation schema past its polemical impasse. |
| [[Joseph Ratzinger]] | Council theology broadly | Peritus for **Frings**; pushed biblical/patristic **ressourcement** (Bonaventure, Augustine). Shared Rahner's reservations on GS Part I's **"astonishing optimism."** |
| [[Hans Küng]] | Reform / collegiality | Progressive peritus; pushed synodal/collegial structures; later censured. |
| [[Jean Daniélou]] | [[Ad Gentes]] / patristic grounding | Grounded texts in the Fathers + missionary theology; later Communio. |

> [!tip] Exam angle
> Two post-conciliar camps grew out of these same men: **Concilium** (Rahner, Küng, Schillebeeckx) vs **Communio** (Ratzinger, de Lubac, von Balthasar). → [[06 - Reception Controversies & Hermeneutics]]
