# Glossary of Latin & Key Terms

> [!summary] One-line
> Every technical term in the vault, defined. Latin unless marked *(It.)* Italian, *(Fr.)* French, *(Gk.)* Greek.

## Core programmatic ideas
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **aggiornamento** *(It.)* | "Updating / bringing up to date." John XXIII's watchword. → [[02 - Vatican II Origin Purpose & Context]] |
| **ressourcement** *(Fr.)* | "Return to the sources" — Scripture & the Fathers. Twin of aggiornamento. |
| **nouvelle théologie** *(Fr.)* | "New theology" — the ressourcement movement (de Lubac, Daniélou, Congar) vs neo-Scholasticism. |
| **reunione** *(It.)* | "Reunion / unity" — John XXIII's second aim, Christian unity. |
| **le surnaturel** *(Fr.)* | "The supernatural" — [[Henri de Lubac]]'s work on nature & grace that got him silenced. |

## Ecclesiology & the Church
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **subsistit in** | "Subsists in" — [[Lumen Gentium]] 8: the Church of Christ *subsists in* the Catholic Church. |
| **semper purificanda** | "Always to be purified" — the Church holy yet ever in need of purification. |
| **semper reformanda** | "Always to be reformed" — cf. [[Unitatis Redintegratio]] 6, continual reform. |
| **Populus Dei** | "People of God" — [[Lumen Gentium]] 9–17. |
| **munus triplex** | The "triple office" — priest, prophet, king — shared by all the baptized. → [[Apostolicam Actuositatem]] |
| **sensus fidelium** | "Sense of the faithful" — the whole People's supernatural instinct for the faith (LG 12). |
| **in persona Christi** | "In the person of Christ" — how the priest acts. → [[Presbyterorum Ordinis]] |
| **communio** | "Communion" — the postconciliar ecclesiology & the [[06 - Reception Controversies & Hermeneutics\|Communio]] journal. |

## Council procedure & law
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **peritus / periti** | "Expert(s)" — the theological advisers. → [[000 - Theologians Index]] |
| **schema / schemata** | "Draft(s)" — the draft documents debated. → [[03 - Preparatory Phase Schemata & Sessions]] |
| **Nota explicativa praevia** | "Preliminary explanatory note" — appended to [[Lumen Gentium]] to safeguard papal primacy. |
| **Coetus Internationalis Patrum** | "International Group of Fathers" — the ~200-bishop conservative bloc. → [[Marcel Lefebvre]] |
| **Semper Idem** | "Always the same" — [[Alfredo Ottaviani]]'s motto. |
| **ad intra / ad extra** | "Inward / outward" — [[Leo Josef Suenens]]'s split of the agenda. |
| **communicatio in sacris** | "Sharing in sacred things" — shared worship with other Christians. → [[Orientalium Ecclesiarum]], [[Unitatis Redintegratio]] |
| **loci theologici** | "Theological sources/places" — [[Marie-Dominique Chenu]]'s "signs of the times" *in act*. |
| **latae sententiae** | "Of a sentence already passed" — automatic excommunication (Lefebvre, 1988). |
| **suspensio a divinis** | "Suspension from things divine" — barred from priestly ministry (Lefebvre, 1976). |
