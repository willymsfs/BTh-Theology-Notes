# Orientalium Ecclesiarum — Decree on the Catholic Eastern Churches

> [!summary] One-line
> Esteem for the **Eastern Catholic Churches**, protecting their heritage against forced Latinization, and groundbreaking rules for **sharing worship** with separated Eastern Christians.

**Type:** Decree · **Promulgated:** 21 Nov 1964 (vote 2,110–39)

## Central idea
High esteem for the Eastern Catholic Churches; preserve their unique heritage; guidelines for sharing worship with non-Catholic Eastern Christians.

## Key points
- Defends against **forced Latinization**; protects rights/prerogatives of Eastern **patriarchs**.
- Different theological formulations are **complementary, not conflicting** (**OE 17**). → feeds [[Unitatis Redintegratio]] "hierarchy of truths"
- Pastoral rules for *communicatio in sacris* (sharing worship) with separated Eastern Christians (OE 26–29).

## Key quote
> *"Eastern Christians who are in fact separated in good Faith from the Catholic Church, if they ask of their own accord and have the right Dispositions, may be admitted to the Sacraments of Penance, the Eucharist and the Anointing of the Sick."* — **OE 27**

## Structure
Patriarchate (7–11) · Liturgical Practice (12–23) · Relations with non-Catholic Eastern Christians (24–25) · Sharing Worship (26–29).
