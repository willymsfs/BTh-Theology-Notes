# Apostolicam Actuositatem — Decree on the Apostolate of the Laity

> [!summary] One-line
> The dogmatic foundation of the **lay apostolate**: laypeople actively share Christ's **triple office** (priest, prophet, king) and evangelize the secular world.

**Type:** Decree · **Promulgated:** 18 Nov 1965 (with [[Dei Verbum]])

## Central idea
Laypeople actively **share in the saving mission** of the Church and in the triple office of Christ.

## Key points
- Laity called to evangelize and apply Christian principles to the **secular world** (social action, youth, temporal affairs).
- The **Eucharist** nourishes the charity driving the apostolate (AA 3).
- Direct **cooperation with the hierarchy**.
- Respond to the **Holy Spirit** (AA 33); loving action for the needy (AA 8).

## Key quote
> *"By sharing in the office of Christ, priest, prophet and king, the laity have their active roles in the life and activity of the Church."* — **AA 10**

## Context
The layman **Patrick Keegan** addressed the Council on this schema — the only layperson ever to do so. → [[04 - Participants]]
