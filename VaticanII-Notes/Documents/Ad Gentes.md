# Ad Gentes — Decree on the Missionary Activity of the Church

> [!summary] One-line
> The Church is **missionary by her very nature**; evangelize and plant the Church among all peoples, adapting to local cultures — good customs "preserved, purified, elevated" (AG 9).

**Type:** Decree · **Promulgated:** 7 Dec 1965 (last day)

## Central idea
The Church is missionary by her very nature, driven by the **Holy Spirit** to evangelize and establish the Church among all peoples.

## Key points
- Theology of missions; flexibility, **adaptation to local cultures**, dialogue with non-Christians.
- Everything good in human customs to be **preserved, purified, elevated** for God's glory (AG 9). → links inculturation to [[Nostra Aetate]]
- Evangelization is the fundamental task of **all** Christians.

## Key quote
> *"It's God's Plan that the Whole Body of the People which makes up the Human Race should form One People of God, should be joined in One Body of Christ, should be built up together in One Temple of the Holy Spirit."* — **AG 7**
