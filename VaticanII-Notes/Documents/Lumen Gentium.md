# Lumen Gentium — Dogmatic Constitution on the Church

> [!summary] One-line
> The dogmatic heart of the Council: the Church as **mystery / sacrament** and **People of God**, with **collegiality**, the **universal call to holiness**, and Mary. Vote **2,151–5**.

**Type:** Constitution · **Promulgated:** 21 Nov 1964 · **Chief redactor:** [[05 - Theologians Influence Map|Gérard Philips]]

## Central idea
Defines the mystery and nature of the Church — shifting from a strictly hierarchical/institutional model to the Church as **"a sacrament or as a sign and instrument both of a very closely knit union with God and of the unity of the whole human race"** (**LG 1**).

## Key themes
- **People of God (LG 9–17):** the new People gathered by the Spirit; a people "one, not according to the flesh but in the Spirit" (LG 9). #people-of-god
- **Hierarchy & collegiality (LG 18–29):** visible society and Mystical Body are "one complex reality." Common and ministerial priesthood "differ… in essence and not only in degree," yet interrelated (LG 10). The **college of bishops with the pope** shares supreme power. #collegiality
- **Universal call to holiness (LG 39–42):** *everyone* — hierarchy and laity — is called to the fullness of Christian life and perfection of charity. #universal-call-to-holiness
- **Mary (LG 52–69):** Mother of God in the mystery of Christ and the Church; "a sign of sure hope and solace to the people of God" (LG 68).

## Key quotes
> *"This Church… subsists in the Catholic Church, which is governed by the successor of Peter and by the bishops in communion with him."* — **LG 8** (the famous *subsistit in*)

> *"The baptized, by regeneration and the anointing of the Holy Spirit, are consecrated as a spiritual house and a holy priesthood."* — **LG 10**

## Controversy
The **Nota explicativa praevia** (appended during "Black Week," Nov 1964) clarified that **collegiality** was not "conciliarism," reinforcing papal primacy. → [[06 - Reception Controversies & Hermeneutics]]
