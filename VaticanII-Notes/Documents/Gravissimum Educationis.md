# Gravissimum Educationis — Declaration on Christian Education

> [!summary] One-line
> A universal **right to education**; Christian education integrated into the whole of human life — "in the world and for the world," defending parents' freedom to choose schools.

**Type:** Declaration · **Promulgated:** 28 Oct 1965

## Central idea
All people have a fundamental **right to education**; Christian education must be woven into the whole pattern of human life, not isolated (GE 1).

## Key points
- Defends **parents' right** to freely choose schools (no state monopoly).
- Church's duty to educate — esp. through **Catholic schools**.
- Freedom of research in Catholic colleges/universities.
- Education "**IN the world and in a sense FOR the world**."

## Key quote
> *"We must work out Our Salvation in the Concrete Situation of the World and contribute to the Whole Human Community of Which Every Human Person is an Integral and Inseparable Part."*
