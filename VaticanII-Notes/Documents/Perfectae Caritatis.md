# Perfectae Caritatis — Decree on the Renewal of Religious Life

> [!summary] One-line
> Renew religious institutes by **returning to the Gospel and the founder's charism** while adapting to contemporary needs.

**Type:** Decree · **Promulgated:** 28 Oct 1965

## Central idea
Renewal of religious life through dynamic adaptation to contemporary needs **while returning to the Gospel and the original charism of the founders**.

## Five principles of renewal (PC 2)
1. The **Gospel** as ultimate norm.
2. Return to the **founder's charism**.
3. Integration into the Church's mission.
4. Prophetic interaction with the world.
5. Life primarily ordered to the **following of Christ**.

Vows of poverty, chastity, obedience tied to the apostolate; religious life is a complete state to be esteemed.

## Key quote
> *"The Gospel is the 'Ultimate Norm', the 'Supreme Law'… The Task of the Religious is to return to the Gospel as lived by the Founder and to articulate that same Gospel in Contemporary Forms."* — **PC 2**
