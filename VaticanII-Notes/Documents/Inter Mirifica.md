# Inter Mirifica — Decree on the Means of Social Communication

> [!summary] One-line
> Media for evangelization and the moral responsibility of laity and hierarchy; the **second** document promulgated (4 Dec 1963).

**Type:** Decree · **Promulgated:** 4 Dec 1963 (with [[Sacrosanctum Concilium]])

## Central idea
The use of modern media/social communication for **evangelization**, and the **moral responsibility** of laity and Church in this sphere.

## Key points
- Laity's responsibility in media; media used to form **sound public opinion** and to evangelize.
- Concrete assistance (personnel, equipment) to missionary countries.
- Proposes an **international Catholic news agency**.

## Core directive
> The Council asks the pope to extend the duties and competence of the Secretariat for the Supervision of Publications and Entertainment to embrace **all media**, including the press, and to appoint experts from various countries, including **laymen**.
