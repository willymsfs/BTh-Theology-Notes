# Presbyterorum Ordinis — Decree on the Ministry and Life of Priests

> [!summary] One-line
> Spirituality and ministry of priests who act *in persona Christi*; **Eucharist is the center**, urged daily. Promulgated on the Council's final day.

**Type:** Decree · **Promulgated:** 7 Dec 1965 (last day)

## Central idea
The spirituality, dignity and active ministry of ordained priests as leaders and servants acting *in persona Christi* for the People of God.

## Key points
- Priests are **set apart but not separated** from the people; lead with humility, obedience, poverty.
- The **Eucharist** is the absolute center of ministry — celebrate **daily** (PO 13).
- Spiritual life and ministry connected to the **missionary** dimension and duties to non-Catholics.

## Key quote
> Priests are *"ordained for them in the things that belong to God, to offer Gifts and Sacrifices for Sins, and to be totally dedicated to the Work for which the Lord has chosen them."* — **PO 3**

## Citations
PO 3 (spirituality) · PO 5 (sacraments tied to Eucharist) · PO 6 (basis of community) · PO 13–14 (daily celebration, deep prayer).
