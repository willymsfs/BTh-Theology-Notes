# Christus Dominus — Decree on the Pastoral Office of Bishops

> [!summary] One-line
> Bishops receive the **fullness of Order** for **service** (Christ's triple office: prophet, priest, pastor); treats collegiality, episcopal conferences, and the bishop–priest bond.

**Type:** Decree · **Promulgated:** 28 Oct 1965

## Central idea
The nature, role and pastoral authority of **bishops**, who receive the fullness of the Sacrament of Order to serve the local and universal Church.

## Key points
- Episcopal authority is for **service** — Christ's triple office of **Prophet, Priest, Pastor**.
- **Collegiality**, internationalization of the Roman Curia, powers of **episcopal conferences**.
- Priests are the bishop's "prudent fellow Workers," dependent on him (**CD 15**).
- Eucharist as center of the local church (CD 30).

## Key quote
> Bishops are *"fortified by the Holy Spirit to call men to believe, or to strengthen an already existing faith."* — **CD 12**
