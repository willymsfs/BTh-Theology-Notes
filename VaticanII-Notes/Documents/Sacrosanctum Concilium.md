# Sacrosanctum Concilium — Constitution on the Sacred Liturgy

> [!summary] One-line
> The **first** document promulgated (4 Dec 1963). Liturgy is the **"source and summit"** of Christian life (SC 10); calls for **active participation**, the **vernacular**, and a richer table of the Word.

**Type:** Constitution · **Promulgated:** 4 Dec 1963 (with [[Inter Mirifica]])

## Central idea
Impart new vigor to Christian life, adapt Church institutions to modern needs, foster ecumenism, and undertake a general **restoration and promotion of the liturgy**. Liturgy is the font from which all the Church's power flows and the summit toward which all activity is directed (**SC 10**).

## Key themes
- **Active participation (SC 48, 50):** rites simplified and made lucid so the faithful take part "conscious of what they are doing, with devotion and full collaboration." #active-participation
- **Vernacular (SC 54, 63):** mother tongue permitted, esp. for readings and common prayer, and in the sacraments.
- **Primacy of the Word (SC 51):** "the treasures of the bible are to be opened up more lavishly" — a richer lectionary over a cycle of years. → links to [[Dei Verbum]]

## Key quotes
> *"The liturgy… most of all in the divine sacrifice of the Eucharist, is the outstanding means whereby the faithful may express in their lives, and manifest to others, the mystery of Christ and the real nature of the true Church."* — **SC 2**

> *"Zeal for the promotion and restoration of the liturgy is rightly held to be a sign of the providential dispositions of God in our time, as a movement of the Holy Spirit in His Church."* — **SC 43**

## Proposed reforms (SC 50–58)
Rites revised & simplified · central place of the Word · Liturgical Year (I, II & A, B, C) · importance of the homily · Prayer of the Faithful · vernacular · Communion under both kinds on occasions · the two parts of the Mass closely connected · new Rite of Concelebration.

## Structure
Intro (1–4) · General Principles (5–46) · The Eucharist (47–58) · Other Sacraments & Sacramentals (59–82) · Divine Office (83–101) · Liturgical Year (102–111) · Sacred Music (112–121) · Sacred Art & Furnishings (122–130).
