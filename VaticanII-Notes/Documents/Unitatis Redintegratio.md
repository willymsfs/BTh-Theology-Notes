# Unitatis Redintegratio — Decree on Ecumenism

> [!summary] One-line
> Restoring unity among all Christians — away from hostility and a mere "return" model, toward **dialogue, humility, charity**; the Church needs **continual reform** (UR 6). Vote **2,137–11**.

**Type:** Decree · **Promulgated:** 21 Nov 1964

## Central idea
Restoration of unity among all Christians, moving from a simple "return" model toward mutual **dialogue, humility and charity**.

## Key points
- Unity ≠ absolute uniformity: respects ecclesial, liturgical, theological **pluralism** (UR 4). #ecumenism
- The **Holy Spirit** gathers all believers into unity of faith (UR 2).
- Encourages *communicatio in sacris* (common worship) in suitable circumstances (UR 15).
- **Hierarchy of truths** (UR 11) — truths vary in their relation to the foundation of faith.

## Key quote
> *"Christ summons the Church, as She goes Her Pilgrim Way, to that continual Reformation of which She always has need, insofar as She is an Institution of Human Beings here on Earth."* — **UR 6**
