# Optatam Totius — Decree on Priestly Training

> [!summary] One-line
> **Holistic formation** (spiritual, intellectual, pastoral) of seminarians as shepherds after Christ — trained for **service, not honors**.

**Type:** Decree · **Promulgated:** 28 Oct 1965

## Central idea
The holistic formation of seminarians — spiritual, intellectual, pastoral — to become true shepherds modeled on Jesus Christ.

## Key points
- Training adapts to modern times; forms candidates for **service** rather than honors.
- Spirit of prayer, obedience, **celibacy**, self-denial (OT 8–10).
- Deep ecclesiastical studies — philosophy and **Thomistic theology** (OT 13–18).
- Pastoral and ongoing formation (OT 19–21).

## Key quote
> Seminarians *"are to be trained to acquire the Spirit of Prayer, learn to defend and strengthen their Vocation, obtain an Increase of Other Virtues and grow in the Zeal to gain All People for Christ."* — **OT 8**
