# Preparatory Phase, the Schemata & the Four Periods

> [!summary] One-line
> 9,300+ suggestions → 70 draft *schemata* (reduced to ~20) drafted by a Curia-dominated preparatory phase; the bishops rejected the defensive drafts at the 1962 opening and rewrote them over **four sessions (1962–65)**, producing **16 documents**.

## Preparatory phase (1959–1962)
- **1959:** Antepreparatory Commission consults bishops and universities worldwide → **9,300+ suggestions**.
- **1960:** Ten preparatory commissions established to draft texts. → see [[04 - Participants]]
- Produced **~70 schemata (drafts)**, reduced to ~20–22 for discussion.
- **Problem:** phase dominated by the **Roman Curia** (Holy Office under **Cardinal Ottaviani**). Drafts were defensive, suspicious of modern biblical/theological scholarship, polemical — reasserting the anti-Modernist posture. #schemata

## The four periods

### First Period — Autumn 1962
- Bishops **halt** the vote to elect commissions from Curial lists (Cardinals **Liénart** & **Frings** intervene, day 1) → elect their own representatives.
- Schema on the **Sources of Revelation** attacked for negative tone; John XXIII **withdraws** it, sets up a **mixed commission** (Ottaviani + Bea) to rewrite → will become [[Dei Verbum]].
- Draft on the Church rejected as **"triumphalistic."**
- **Ends with no documents promulgated** — but Curial drafts discarded; new pastoral direction set.

### Second Period — Autumn 1963
- **John XXIII dies (3 June 1963)**; **Paul VI** reconvenes, sets **four purposes**: define the Church's nature, renew it, restore unity, dialogue with the world.
- Heavy debate on the Church: **collegiality vs papal authority**.
- **First two documents promulgated (4 Dec 1963):** [[Sacrosanctum Concilium]] + [[Inter Mirifica]].

### Third Period — Autumn 1964
- Tackles **religious freedom** and the Church-and-world question.
- **"Black Week"** — conservative procedural interventions (incl. *Nota Praevia*). → [[06 - Reception Controversies & Hermeneutics]]
- **Promulgated (21 Nov 1964):** [[Lumen Gentium]], [[Unitatis Redintegratio]], [[Orientalium Ecclesiarum]].

### Fourth Period — Autumn 1965
- Completes the agenda: [[Dei Verbum]], [[Gaudium et Spes]], [[Dignitatis Humanae]], [[Nostra Aetate]] and the rest.
- **Council solemnly closed 8 December 1965.**
