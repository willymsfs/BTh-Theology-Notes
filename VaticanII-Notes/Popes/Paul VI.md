# Pope St Paul VI (1897–1978)

> [!summary] One-line
> **"The Master Builder"** — continued the Council after John XXIII, set its **four purposes**, appointed the **four Moderators**, and held majority and minority together (Nota Praevia, Black Week); lifted the **1054 excommunications** with Athenagoras; closed the Council 8 Dec 1965; later *Humanae Vitae* (1968).

**Reign:** 21 Jun 1963 – 1978 · **Role:** the **Master Builder**

## Background
Giovanni Battista Montini — Archbishop of Milan (celebrated the **Ambrosian rite**); long a **curial diplomat** before falling out of favor with Pius XII. Elected **21 Jun 1963**.

## Continuing the Council — the four purposes (session 2, 29 Sep 1963)
1. **Define the nature of the Church and the role of the bishop.**
2. **Renew the Church.**
3. **Restore unity among all Christians** (incl. seeking pardon for Catholic contributions to separation).
4. **Start a dialogue with the contemporary world.**

## Steering the debates
Reformed procedure by appointing **four Moderators** — Agagianian, Lercaro, Döpfner, **[[Leo Josef Suenens]]** — to direct the general congregations.

## Key interventions — "Black Week" (14–21 Nov 1964)
- **Nota Praevia on [[Lumen Gentium]]:** mandated the *Nota explicativa praevia* — the bishops' college acts "only from time to time and only with the consent of its head" → protects **papal primacy** vs collegiality worry.
- **Ecumenism decree:** personally added **19 last-minute modifications** the fathers had to accept next day. → [[Unitatis Redintegratio]]
- **Reserved topics:** withdrew **artificial birth control** from the Council — "not allowed to discuss that at the Council." *(Sources detail birth control; celibacy widely attested but not in this notebook.)*
→ [[06 - Reception Controversies & Hermeneutics]]

## Ecumenism & closing
- **Jan 1964:** unprecedented journey to the **Holy Land** to meet Orthodox Patriarch **Athenagoras**.
- **7 Dec 1965:** joint declaration **lifting the mutual excommunications of 1054** (Great Schism).
- **8 Dec 1965:** closed the Council with the brief *In Spiritu Sancto*. Instituted the **Synod of Bishops** to keep episcopal collaboration: *"Now you're leaving and we feel alone."*

## After the Council
Swift implementation commissions; led liturgical reform **by example** — vernacular Italian Mass facing the people (Mar 1965). His 1968 encyclical ***Humanae Vitae*** (reaffirming the ban on artificial contraception) triggered a **major crisis of papal authority**.

> [!note] Source flag
> **Canonized 14 Oct 2018** by Francis — widely documented but **not** in the NotebookLM sources (which detail his role but not his canonization).

## Legacy
*"It was precisely the credit of Pope Paul VI to have managed to keep all this together"* — securing overwhelming majorities for all **16 documents**.
