# Pope St John XXIII (1881–1963)

> [!summary] One-line
> **"Good Pope John"** — elected as a "transitional" pope at 77, instead **called Vatican II** (25 Jan 1959) on a vision of **aggiornamento + unity**; opened it with *Gaudet Mater Ecclesia* ("medicine of mercy," "prophets of gloom"); died between sessions 1 and 2. Canonized 2014.

**Reign:** 28 Oct 1958 – 3 Jun 1963 · **Role:** the **Architect** of the Council

## Background
Angelo Giuseppe Roncalli, b. 1881, Lombardy farming family. WWI military chaplain, church historian, wartime papal diplomat, then Patriarch of Venice. Elected Pope **28 Oct 1958**, just shy of 77 — cardinals expected a **"transitional pope."** Known as **"Good Pope John" / "the People's Pope"**: pastoral compassion, wit; first Christmas visiting the sick and Regina Coeli prison inmates.

## Calling the Council (25 Jan 1959)
<100 days in, at St Paul Outside the Walls, announced three initiatives: a Roman diocesan synod, revision of the Code of Canon Law, and an **ecumenical council**. Met with **absolute silence** — the Curia thought councils had ended with [[01 - Ecumenical Councils History|Vatican I]]. He saw unprecedented shifts (Cold War, political atheism, atomic age) demanding a bold approach.

## Vision — aggiornamento + reunione
> *"I expect a little fresh air from it… We must shake off the imperial dust that has accumulated on the throne of Saint Peter since Constantine."*

Steered away from new dogmas/anathemas → a **pastoral** council. #aggiornamento

## Opening address — *Gaudet Mater Ecclesia* (11 Oct 1962)
- **"Prophets of gloom"** — rebuked those who "can see nothing but prevarication and ruin."
- **"Medicine of mercy"** — *"the Spouse of Christ prefers to make use of the medicine of mercy rather than that of severity."*
- Church as **"loving mother of all."**
→ [[02 - Vatican II Origin Purpose & Context]]

## Key intervention — session 1
Council deadlocked on the **Divine Revelation** schema (majority opposed but lacked 2/3 to reject). John XXIII **withdrew** it and set up a **mixed commission** under polar opposites **[[Alfredo Ottaviani]] + [[Augustin Bea]]** → eventually [[Dei Verbum]].

## Encyclicals
- ***Mater et Magistra*** (1961) — social justice, defense of the poor.
- ***Pacem in Terris*** (1963) — first encyclical addressed **"to all men of good will"**; human rights and world peace.

## Death & legacy
Died of stomach cancer **3 Jun 1963**, between sessions 1 and 2 — the Council auto-suspended; [[Paul VI]] continued it. The **"Father of Vatican II."** **Canonized 27 Apr 2014** by Francis: *"He was the Pope of openness to the Spirit."*
