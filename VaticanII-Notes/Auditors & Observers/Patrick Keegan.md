# Patrick Keegan

> [!summary] One-line
> English lay leader; the **only layperson ever to address the assembly of bishops** at Vatican II — spoke on the schema on the laity, embodying the new theology of the laity as **co-responsible** for Christ's mission.

**Role:** the only layperson to address the Council

## The address
"Patrick Keegan addressed the bishops on the connection between the **schema on the Church** and the **working document on the laity**." His speech practically demonstrated the Council's emerging theology: the laity are **"co-responsible with Bishops, Priests and Religious for Christ's Mission on Earth."** → [[Apostolicam Actuositatem]] · [[Lumen Gentium]]
