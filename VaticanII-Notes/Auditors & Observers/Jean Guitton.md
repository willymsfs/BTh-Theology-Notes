# Jean Guitton

> [!summary] One-line
> French Catholic layman; the **first lay auditor** at the Council, invited by [[John XXIII]] to the first period — the historic precedent for lay presence at the Church's highest assembly.

**Role:** first lay auditor · invited by [[John XXIII]]

## Significance
Attended the latter part of the Council's **first period** — a historic turning point setting the precedent for lay involvement. Building on John XXIII's initiative, [[Paul VI]] later "provided for the attendance of several lay auditors at the general congregations and for their assistance to the commissions." At the start of the **second period**, Guitton was joined by **ten other laymen** from various countries, largely from international Catholic organizations.
