# John Moorman (Anglican, Bishop of Ripon)

> [!summary] One-line
> Chief **Anglican observer** (1962–65); Franciscan historian, fluent in Italian; declared Rome had "at last begun to interest herself in the problem of unity"; later chaired the commission that led to **ARCIC**.

**Role:** chief Anglican observer · **Life:** 1905–1989 · Bishop of Ripon

## At the Council
Expert on St Francis and Franciscan history; used his knowledge and Italian to contribute. Insisted on a distinct Anglican voice:
> *"The Anglicans must make their own contribution rather than allow the Methodists, Congregationalists and others to speak on behalf of them."*

## His famous verdict
> *"Rome has, at last, begun to interest herself in the problem of unity, and things can never be the same again… A new pattern has emerged as a result of the Council, and much of the thought and language which was valid five years ago is now obsolete."*

## Legacy
1967: chaired the Anglican commission that led to the **Anglican-Roman Catholic International Commission (ARCIC)**. → [[Unitatis Redintegratio]]
