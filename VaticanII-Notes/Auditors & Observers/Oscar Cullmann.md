# Oscar Cullmann (Lutheran observer)

> [!summary] One-line
> Prominent **Lutheran NT scholar** and observer; held the Council's greatest ecumenical potential lay in its **rediscovery of Scripture**; read Vatican II as an ongoing **"event,"** rejecting both "revolutionary" and "continuist" readings.

**Role:** Lutheran observer · **Life:** 1902–1999 · New Testament scholar

## His theological reading
- The **rediscovery of Scripture and biblical hermeneutics** was VII's greatest ecumenical potential — the Catholic Church's return to biblical origins. → [[Dei Verbum]]
- Rejected **both** the "revolutionary" and strictly "continuist" interpretations:
> *"From an historical point of view, Vatican II managed to renew Catholicism… without changing the fundamentals of the Catholic Church…"*
- Noted the **progressive majority dominated** from beginning to end.
- Council as ongoing **"event"** with lasting momentum:
> *"More than in any other council here it was about the whole event of the council, whose impulses were active in the future not less than the final texts."*
