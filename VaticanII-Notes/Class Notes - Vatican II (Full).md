## PART I — THE ECUMENICAL COUNCILS

### 1.1 What is an ecumenical council?
A worldwide assembly of bishops, patriarchs, cardinals and hierarchs gathered to decide matters of **faith** and **discipline**. To be valid and *truly ecumenical* it must be **convened and presided over by the Pope (or his legates)** and its decisions **ratified by the Pope**. Classic purpose: **define doctrine, condemn heresy (anathemas), state official teaching.**

John XXIII: a council is a *"solemn celebration of the union of Christ and His Church."*

### 1.2 The 21 councils at a glance
- **Antiquity (1–8)** · **Middle Ages / Papal (9–15)** · **Conciliarism + Lateran V (16–18)** · **Modern: Trent (19), Vatican I (20), Vatican II (21).**
- The **first four** are the Christological/Trinitarian bedrock:
  - **Nicaea I (325)** — Creed; consubstantiality of the Son; vs Arianism.
  - **Constantinople I (381)** — divinity of the Holy Spirit.
  - **Ephesus (431)** — Mary *Theotokos*; vs Nestorianism.
  - **Chalcedon (451)** — two natures, one person.

### 1.3 The two modern predecessors
- **Trent (1545–63):** answered the Reformation; defined Scripture & Tradition, original sin, justification, sacraments; Tridentine Mass (1570).
- **Vatican I (1869–70):** a "fortress" against liberalism, revolution, Marxism; defined **papal infallibility** (*Pastor Aeternus*); cut short by the fall of the Papal States — its ecclesiology **left unfinished**.

### 1.4 Significance & impact of councils
Councils secure doctrinal unity, settle disputes, reform discipline, and articulate the Church's self-understanding for each age. Vatican II's impact: it changed the Church's **self-image from fortress to pilgrim People of God**, from condemnation to dialogue.

## PART II — VATICAN II: ORIGIN, PURPOSE, CONTEXT

### 2.1 The announcement
**25 January 1959** — John XXIII, <100 days after election, announces a council at St Paul Outside the Walls. A shock: many thought Vatican I had made further councils superfluous.

### 2.2 Context — the 1960s
Post-WWII and Holocaust; Cold War and Soviet atheism; decolonization, globalization, the information age. Inside the Church: **biblical, liturgical, ecumenical, and patristic** revival movements — and a clash between **neo-Scholastic** conservatism and the **Nouvelle Théologie**.

### 2.3 The two engines
- **Aggiornamento** — updating. *"I expect a little fresh air… we must shake off the imperial dust that has accumulated on the throne of Saint Peter since Constantine."*
- **Ressourcement** — return to the sources (Scripture + Fathers). *Aggiornamento is impossible without ressourcement.*

### 2.4 A pastoral council
No anathemas. *"Christ's Bride prefers the Balm of Mercy to the Arm of Severity."* Three goals: **spiritual renewal, Christian unity, dialogue with the modern world.** Opened **11 Oct 1962** with *Gaudet Mater Ecclesia* ("prophets of gloom" rejected).

## PART III — PREPARATION, SCHEMATA, SESSIONS

### 3.1 Preparatory phase
1959 consultation → **9,300+ suggestions**; ten commissions (1960) drafted **~70 schemata** (reduced to ~20). Problem: **Curia-dominated** (Ottaviani / Holy Office), the drafts defensive and anti-Modernist.

### 3.2 The four periods
1. **1962** — bishops reject Curial control (Liénart, Frings); revelation schema withdrawn; "triumphalistic" Church draft rejected; **no documents promulgated.**
2. **1963** — John XXIII dies (3 Jun); Paul VI continues (four purposes). **SC + IM promulgated (4 Dec 1963).**
3. **1964** — religious freedom & world debated; **"Black Week"**; **LG, UR, OE promulgated (21 Nov 1964).**
4. **1965** — DV, GS, DH, NA etc. completed; **Council closed 8 Dec 1965.**

## PART IV — PARTICIPANTS

- **Popes:** John XXIII (Architect) · Paul VI (Master Builder).
- **Majority:** Liénart, Frings, Suenens, Alfrink, König, Döpfner.
- **Minority:** Ottaviani, Siri, Ruffini.
- **Periti:** Congar, Chenu, Schillebeeckx, Rahner, Küng (progressive); Ratzinger, de Lubac, Daniélou (ressourcement); Murray (religious liberty); Philips (LG redactor).
- **Laity:** Jean Guitton; **Patrick Keegan** (only layperson to address the Council); women auditors from 1964.
- **Observers:** Moorman (Anglican), Schlink/Cullmann/Barth (Protestant), Orthodox (Moscow, then Constantinople).

**2,908 Council Fathers** — the largest, first truly global council.

## PART VI — THE 16 DOCUMENTS (date-wise)

> [!info] Promulgation sequence
> **4 Dec 1963:** SC, IM · **21 Nov 1964:** LG, OE, UR · **28 Oct 1965:** CD, PC, OT, GE, NA · **18 Nov 1965:** DV, AA · **7 Dec 1965:** DH, GS, AG, PO.
> Contested votes: **DH 1,954–249** and **GS 2,111–251**.

### The 4 Constitutions
- **[[Sacrosanctum Concilium]]** — liturgy as "source and summit" (SC 10); active participation (SC 48,50); vernacular (SC 54,63); richer Word (SC 51).
- **[[Lumen Gentium]]** — Church as sacrament (LG 1) & People of God (LG 9); *subsistit in* (LG 8); collegiality (LG 18–29); universal call to holiness (LG 39–42); Mary (LG 52–69).
- **[[Dei Verbum]]** — revelation as self-gift (DV 1); Scripture+Tradition one deposit, Magisterium serves the Word (DV 10); salvific inerrancy (DV 11); "soul of theology" (DV 24).
- **[[Gaudium et Spes]]** — Church serves the world (GS 4); signs of the times (GS 44); dignity, marriage, culture, economics; peace (GS 78,80); opening line (GS 1).

### The 9 Decrees
- **[[Inter Mirifica]]** — media for evangelization.
- **[[Orientalium Ecclesiarum]]** — Eastern Catholic Churches; no forced Latinization; shared worship (OE 27).
- **[[Unitatis Redintegratio]]** — ecumenism; continual reform (UR 6); hierarchy of truths (UR 11).
- **[[Christus Dominus]]** — bishops' pastoral office; service; collegiality (CD 12,15).
- **[[Perfectae Caritatis]]** — renewal of religious life; Gospel + founder's charism (PC 2).
- **[[Optatam Totius]]** — priestly formation; holistic training (OT 8).
- **[[Presbyterorum Ordinis]]** — priests; Eucharist the center (PO 3,13).
- **[[Apostolicam Actuositatem]]** — lay apostolate; triple office (AA 10).
- **[[Ad Gentes]]** — mission by nature; inculturation (AG 7,9).

### The 3 Declarations
- **[[Gravissimum Educationis]]** — right to education; parents' freedom (GE 1).
- **[[Nostra Aetate]]** — non-Christian religions; "rejects nothing true and holy" (NA 2); rejects "deicide"/anti-Semitism (NA 4).
- **[[Dignitatis Humanae]]** — religious liberty; immunity from coercion (DH 2).

## PART VII — RECEPTION & CONTROVERSY

- **During:** "Black Week" (Nov 1964) — *Nota Praevia* on LG, postponed DH vote, ecumenism edits.
- **After:** **hermeneutic of continuity/reform (Benedict XVI) vs rupture.** Schools: **Concilium** (Rahner, Küng, Schillebeeckx) vs **Communio** (Ratzinger, de Lubac, von Balthasar); **Bologna** (Alberigo — Council as "event").
- **Rejection:** **Lefebvre / SSPX** — rejected DH ("legalization of apostasy") and the liturgy; 1988 schism.
- **GS optimism** dispute; **NA** & Jewish relations; the **"spirit of the council"** debate.
