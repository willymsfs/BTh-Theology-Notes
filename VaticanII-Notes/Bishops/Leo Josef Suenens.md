# Cardinal Leo Josef Suenens (Malines-Brussels)

> [!summary] One-line
> Archbishop of Malines-Brussels; one of Paul VI's **four Moderators**; gave the Council its structure — Church ***ad intra* / *ad extra*** — the conceptual birthplace of [[Gaudium et Spes]]; later patron of the Charismatic Renewal.

**See:** Malines-Brussels, Belgium · **Bloc:** progressive majority · **Role:** Moderator (with Agagianian, Lercaro, Döpfner)

## Structuring the agenda — *ad intra* / *ad extra*
March 1962: "a very powerful appeal for some order." Proposed the Council center on the **Church**, in two parts:
- **Ad intra** — inward: hierarchy, laity, liturgy.
- **Ad extra** — outward: the Church's relationship to the world.
Reiterated to the full assembly later.

## Championing Gaudium et Spes
The *ad extra* focus was the conceptual **birthplace of [[Gaudium et Spes]]** (Schema 13). Suenens remained its primary champion, fighting to make the Council address contemporary society.

## Women auditors & Charismatic Renewal
> [!note] Source flag (outside the NotebookLM sources)
> Widely documented history: Suenens **campaigned on the Council floor for the inclusion of women**, asking why they were absent; and after the Council he became the leading **episcopal protector of the Catholic Charismatic Renewal**. The NotebookLM notebook confirms women auditors were invited at the third session but does not name Suenens for these two points.
