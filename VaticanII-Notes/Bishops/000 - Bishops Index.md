# Bishops & Cardinals of Vatican II

> [!summary] One-line
> The Council floor split into a reforming **majority** and a defensive **minority** — plus the traditionalist bloc that broke away.

## Majority (reformers)
- [[Josef Frings]] — Cologne; day-one intervention; Ratzinger's patron
- [[Leo Josef Suenens]] — Malines-Brussels; Moderator; *ad intra / ad extra*
- **Achille Liénart** (Lille) — co-mover of the day-one intervention
- **Bernard Alfrink** (Utrecht), **Franz König** (Vienna), **Julius Döpfner** (Munich) — transalpine leaders

## Minority (conservatives)
- [[Alfredo Ottaviani]] — Holy Office; *Semper Idem*; defensive schemata
- **Giuseppe Siri** (Genoa), **Ernesto Ruffini** (Palermo)

## Traditionalist bloc → schism
- [[Marcel Lefebvre]] — *Coetus Internationalis Patrum*; SSPX (1970); 1988 schism

## Fault line
| | Majority | Minority |
|---|---|---|
| Mood | aggiornamento, dialogue | *Semper Idem*, defense |
| Key wins | freed commissions, pastoral turn | *Nota Praevia*, "Black Week" delays |
| Documents | LG, GS, DH, UR | resisted DH, collegiality, liturgy |
