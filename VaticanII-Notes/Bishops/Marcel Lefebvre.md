# Archbishop Marcel Lefebvre (1905–1991)

> [!summary] One-line
> Former Abp of Dakar, Superior General of the Holy Ghost Fathers; leader-figure of the **Coetus Internationalis Patrum**; rejected **religious liberty, ecumenism, collegiality**; founded the **SSPX (1970)**; **1988 illicit consecrations → automatic excommunication / schism**.

**Role:** former Abp of Dakar; Superior General, Congregation of the Holy Spirit · **Bloc:** *Coetus Internationalis Patrum*

## At the Council
Appointed by John XXIII to the central preparatory commission, but became a persistent **critic of the majority**. Aligned with the **Coetus Internationalis Patrum** — an informal bloc of ~200 conservative bishops organized to resist the Council's direction.

## What he rejected
- **Liturgical reform**, **ecumenism**, **episcopal collegiality** (as contrary to hierarchical/ultramontanist tradition).
- Voted **against [[Dignitatis Humanae]]** (7 Dec 1965).
> *"Religious liberty is the major aim of Liberalism. Liberals, Masons, and Protestants are fully aware that by this means they can strike at the very heart of the Catholic Church."*
- Held the Council was infiltrated by a **"liberal-Protestant-Masonic conspiracy"** — its pastoral style a Trojan horse for modernism.

## SSPX & the schism
- Championed the **"Mass of all times"** against the **"Mass of our time."**
- **1970:** founded the **Society of St Pius X (SSPX)**.
- **1976:** penalized — **suspension *a divinis*** (the notebook loosely says "excommunication in 1976"; correct canonical term is *suspension*).
- **1988:** **illicitly consecrated four bishops** without papal approval → incurred **automatic excommunication (*latae sententiae*)** → formal schism.
