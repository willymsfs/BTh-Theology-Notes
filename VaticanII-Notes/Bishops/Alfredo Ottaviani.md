# Cardinal Alfredo Ottaviani

> [!summary] One-line
> **Prefect of the Holy Office**, head of the Doctrinal Commission; leader of the **conservative minority** under the motto ***Semper Idem*** ("always the same"); author of the defensive schemata; clashed with **Bea** and **Frings**.

**Role:** Prefect, Holy Office · head of the **Doctrinal / Theological Commission** · **Motto:** *Semper Idem* · **Bloc:** conservative minority

## Power over the initial agenda
Steered the Council toward a strictly doctrinal focus. His commission's **schemata** were **defensive, suspicious of modern thought**, designed to complete the **unfinished agenda of [[01 - Ecumenical Councils History|Vatican I]]** — subsuming John XXIII's pastoral aim into polemical, triumphalist drafts. → [[03 - Preparatory Phase Schemata & Sessions]]

## Clashes
- **vs [[Augustin Bea]]** — vied over the Council's agenda/outlook; peaked over the **sources of revelation** debate → Pope formed a **mixed commission co-chaired by Ottaviani and Bea** to rewrite the text ([[Dei Verbum]]).
- **vs [[Josef Frings]]** — after Frings criticized the Holy Office's methods, Ottaviani "vehemently defended" it — showing his difficulty accepting the Council's authority **above** the Holy Office.

## Source flag — the 1969 "Ottaviani Intervention"
> [!note] Outside the NotebookLM sources
> Widely documented: in **1969** Ottaviani co-signed a *Short Critical Study of the New Order of Mass* ("the Ottaviani Intervention") objecting to the Novus Ordo. **Not** in the notebook — verify independently before teaching.
