# Cardinal Josef Frings (Cologne)

> [!summary] One-line
> Archbishop of Cologne; leader of the **progressive majority**; the **day-one intervention** (with Liénart) that freed the Council from Curial control; brought **Ratzinger** as his peritus; publicly clashed with **Ottaviani** over the Holy Office.

**See:** Cologne, Germany · **Bloc:** progressive "transalpine" (with Alfrink, König) · **Peritus:** [[Joseph Ratzinger]]

## Day-one intervention (13 Oct 1962)
Curia planned an immediate vote to fill conciliar commissions from **pre-approved insider lists**. Card. **Liénart** moved to postpone so bishops could nominate their own; *"he was backed by Cardinal Frings — a Franco-German alliance — and there was a burst of applause."* → made the Council a **deliberative body**, not a rubber stamp. → [[03 - Preparatory Phase Schemata & Sessions]]

## Clash with Ottaviani (Nov 1963)
During a debate on diocesan governance, Frings — reading slowly from huge notes (poor eyesight) — **openly criticized the methods of the Holy Office**. The atmosphere in St Peter's turned *"electric"*; [[Alfredo Ottaviani]] vehemently defended his office.
> [!note] Source flag
> The famous line "its methods are a **scandal**" is historically attributed to this speech but is **not** quoted in the NotebookLM sources — the sources say only that he "frankly criticized the methods of the Holy Office."

## Ratzinger connection
Frings asked the young Bonn theologian [[Joseph Ratzinger]] to be his adviser; officially appointed **peritus** — helped shape majority speeches and texts.
