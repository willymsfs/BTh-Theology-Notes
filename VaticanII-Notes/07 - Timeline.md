# Vatican II — Chronological Timeline

> [!summary] One-line
> From Roncalli's election (**28 Oct 1958**) to the solemn close (**8 Dec 1965**) — four working periods, two popes, sixteen documents.

## Pre-council
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 1869–1870 | **[[01 - Ecumenical Councils History\|Vatican I]]** — cut short, ecclesiology left unfinished |
| **28 Oct 1958** | Angelo Roncalli elected **[[John XXIII]]** (expected "transitional") |
| **25 Jan 1959** | John XXIII **announces** the Council (St Paul Outside the Walls) — met with silence |
| 1959 | Antepreparatory consultation → **9,300+ suggestions** |
| 1960 (5 Jun) | **Ten preparatory commissions** + secretariats established |
| 1961 | Encyclical *Mater et Magistra* |
| 25 Dec 1961 | Council **formally convoked** |
| → 1962 | Curia-dominated drafting of **~70 [[03 - Preparatory Phase Schemata & Sessions\|schemata]]** |

## Period I — Autumn 1962
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| **11 Oct 1962** | Council **opens**; *[[John XXIII\|Gaudet Mater Ecclesia]]* ("medicine of mercy") |
| 13 Oct 1962 | **Day-one intervention** — [[Josef Frings]] & Liénart free the commissions |
| Autumn 1962 | Revelation schema attacked → John XXIII **withdraws** it; mixed commission ([[Alfredo Ottaviani]] + [[Augustin Bea]]) |
| 8 Dec 1962 | Period I ends — **no documents promulgated**, Curial drafts discarded |

## Between periods
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| Apr 1963 | Encyclical *Pacem in Terris* ("to all men of good will") |
| **3 Jun 1963** | **[[John XXIII]] dies** → Council auto-suspended |
| **21 Jun 1963** | **[[Paul VI]]** elected; pledges to continue |

## Period II — Autumn 1963
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 29 Sep 1963 | Paul VI opens Period II; sets the **four purposes** |
| Autumn 1963 | Debate: collegiality vs papal authority |
| **4 Dec 1963** | ✅ **[[Sacrosanctum Concilium]]** + **[[Inter Mirifica]]** promulgated |

## Period III — Autumn 1964
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| Jan 1964 | Paul VI meets Patriarch **Athenagoras** in the Holy Land |
| Autumn 1964 | Women auditors admitted (8 religious + 7 lay) |
| **14–21 Nov 1964** | **"Black Week"** — *Nota Praevia* on LG; DH vote postponed; ecumenism edits |
| **21 Nov 1964** | ✅ **[[Lumen Gentium]]** · **[[Orientalium Ecclesiarum]]** · **[[Unitatis Redintegratio]]** |

## Period IV — Autumn 1965
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 15 Sep 1965 | Paul VI institutes the **Synod of Bishops** |
| **28 Oct 1965** | ✅ **[[Christus Dominus]]** · **[[Perfectae Caritatis]]** · **[[Optatam Totius]]** · **[[Gravissimum Educationis]]** · **[[Nostra Aetate]]** |
| **18 Nov 1965** | ✅ **[[Dei Verbum]]** · **[[Apostolicam Actuositatem]]** |
| **7 Dec 1965** | ✅ **[[Dignitatis Humanae]]** · **[[Gaudium et Spes]]** · **[[Ad Gentes]]** · **[[Presbyterorum Ordinis]]**; **1054 excommunications lifted** with Athenagoras |
| **8 Dec 1965** | Council **solemnly closed** — brief *In Spiritu Sancto* |

## After
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 1968 | Paul VI — *Humanae Vitae* (crisis of authority) |
| 1970 | [[Marcel Lefebvre]] founds the **SSPX** |
| 1988 | Lefebvre's illicit consecrations → excommunication / schism |
| 2005 | Benedict XVI's **hermeneutic of reform** address → [[06 - Reception Controversies & Hermeneutics]] |
| 2014 / 2018 | [[John XXIII]] / [[Paul VI]] canonized |
