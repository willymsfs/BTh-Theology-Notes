# Hans Küng

> [!summary] One-line
> Swiss theologian, Tübingen; progressive peritus pushing **synodal/collegial** reform; co-founded **Concilium**; later **stripped of his license** to teach Catholic theology under John Paul II.

**Tendency:** progressive/liberal · **Journal:** [[06 - Reception Controversies & Hermeneutics|Concilium]]

## At the Council
Influential peritus for the progressive majority; strong advocate of **synodal and collegial structures** to root the Council's reforms.

## Post-conciliar & censure
Co-founded **Concilium** (1964) with [[Yves Congar]], [[Karl Rahner]], [[Edward Schillebeeckx]] — to perpetuate the "spirit of the council." His increasingly liberal reading ("anticipating Vatican four") caused a deep **rift with [[Joseph Ratzinger]] and [[Henri de Lubac]]** (→ Communio). Under **John Paul II** he faced official censure — the Pope **"takes away his license to teach Catholic theology."**
