# Cardinal Augustin Bea, SJ

> [!summary] One-line
> Biblical scholar; head of the **Secretariat for Christian Unity**; co-chaired (with Ottaviani) the mixed commission that reworked [[Dei Verbum]]; architect of **[[Unitatis Redintegratio]]** and **[[Nostra Aetate]]** (esp. Jewish relations).

**Role:** Head of the Secretariat for the Promotion of Christian Unity (appointed by John XXIII)

## At the Council
Frequently **clashed with Ottaviani** (Holy Office) over the agenda and theological direction.

## Documents shaped
- **[[Dei Verbum]]:** after the defensive "sources of revelation" draft was criticized, John XXIII formed a **mixed commission** — Bea **joint chairman** with Ottaviani — that completely reworked the text.
- **[[Unitatis Redintegratio]]:** his Secretariat drove the ecumenism decree.
- **[[Nostra Aetate]]:** his work reframed the Church's relation to the **Jewish people** and **decried anti-Semitism**; also championed religious freedom.
