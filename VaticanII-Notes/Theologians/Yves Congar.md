# Yves Congar, OP

> [!summary] One-line
> French Dominican peritus; the great theologian of the **laity**, **People of God**, and **collegiality/communion**; a **Trinitarian** ecclesiology feeding [[Lumen Gentium]] and [[Ad Gentes]]. Co-founded **Concilium** yet held a moderate line.

**Order:** Dominican · **Tendency:** progressive neo-Thomist

## Contribution
- Champion of the **laity** and the **"People of God"** — shaped the Church's participatory mission.
- Noted Vatican II's ecclesiology is far more **Trinitarian** than Vatican I's.
- Used **Aquinas** to develop **Church-as-communion** and the theological foundations of **episcopal collegiality**.
- Influenced **[[Lumen Gentium]]** (People of God, collegiality) and **[[Ad Gentes]]** (missionary People of God).

## Post-conciliar
Among the founders of **[[06 - Reception Controversies & Hermeneutics|Concilium]]** (with [[Karl Rahner]], [[Hans Küng]], [[Edward Schillebeeckx]]), though generally read as a moderate elder of the reform.
