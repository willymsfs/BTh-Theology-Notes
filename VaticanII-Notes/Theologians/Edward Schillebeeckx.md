# Edward Schillebeeckx, OP

> [!summary] One-line
> Dutch Dominican; theologian of **sacraments, Christology, ministry** and the **Episcopal College/collegiality**; pushed the Church to "redefine its position toward the secularized world" → [[Gaudium et Spes]]; co-founded **Concilium**; later questioned by the CDF.

**Order:** Dutch Dominican · **Tendency:** progressive neo-Thomist · **Journal:** [[06 - Reception Controversies & Hermeneutics|Concilium]]

## Method
Theology begins with a "global initiation in salvation history"; philosophy "centered on man, his world and God." Strong positive appreciation of modern philosophy.

## Contributions
- **Collegiality:** emphasized the **role of the Episcopal College** and the principle of collegiality. → [[Lumen Gentium]], [[Christus Dominus]]
- **[[Gaudium et Spes]] (Schema 13):** argued the Church must **"redefine its position toward the secularized world"** and "solemnly bear witness" to this new self-awareness.
- **Dutch Catechism** (post-council) — tied to his sphere; a flashpoint that exposed conciliar divisions and spurred the rival journals.

## Tensions & post-conciliar
Survived pre-VII suppression. Represented a **more liberal reading** seeking cultural accommodation → rift with conservatives. Under John Paul II the **CDF questioned** his theology. **Founding architect of Concilium** — co-wrote (with [[Karl Rahner]]) the 1965 inaugural: "a new theology is taking shape."
