# Periti & Theologians of Vatican II

> [!summary] One-line
> The *periti* could not vote but authored the texts. Two streams: **progressive neo-Thomist** (→ Concilium) and **ressourcement neo-Augustinian** (→ Communio).

## By tendency
### Progressive / Neo-Thomist → [[06 - Reception Controversies & Hermeneutics|Concilium]] (1964/65)
- [[Karl Rahner]] — transcendental method, anonymous Christianity, LG 16
- [[Yves Congar]] — laity, People of God, collegiality
- [[Marie-Dominique Chenu]] — signs of the times
- [[Edward Schillebeeckx]] — sacraments, collegiality, secularization
- [[Hans Küng]] — reform; later censured

### Ressourcement / Neo-Augustinian → [[06 - Reception Controversies & Hermeneutics|Communio]] (1972)
- [[Henri de Lubac]] — nouvelle théologie, People of God, nature/grace
- [[Joseph Ratzinger]] — Bonaventure/Augustine, Dei Verbum, 2005 hermeneutic
- [[Jean Daniélou]] — patristic + missionary ressourcement

### Redactors & churchmen
- [[Gerard Philips]] — chief redactor of [[Lumen Gentium]]
- [[Augustin Bea]] — Secretariat for Unity; Dei Verbum, UR, NA
- [[John Courtney Murray]] — [[Dignitatis Humanae]]
