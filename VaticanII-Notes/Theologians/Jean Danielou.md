# Jean Daniélou, SJ

> [!summary] One-line
> French Jesuit, leader of **nouvelle théologie**/ressourcement; peritus grounding the documents in **patristic** sources and **missionary** theology; later allied with **Communio** against liberal drift.

**Order:** Jesuit · **Tendency:** neo-Augustinian / ressourcement

## Role
Leading figure of **nouvelle théologie** — return to the Bible and early Church Fathers against rigid neo-Scholasticism. Worked behind the scenes as peritus to ground the documents in **early Christian sources** rather than defensive anti-modernist rhetoric. Emphasis on **missionary theology**. → [[Ad Gentes]]

## Post-conciliar
In the neo-Augustinian stream with [[Henri de Lubac]], von Balthasar, [[Joseph Ratzinger]]. Contributed to the progressive majority at the Council but grew **skeptical** of liberal accommodation; allied with the **Communio** middle path — ressourcement without abandoning classical Christianity.
