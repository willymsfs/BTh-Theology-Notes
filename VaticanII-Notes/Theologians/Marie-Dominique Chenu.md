# Marie-Dominique Chenu, OP

> [!summary] One-line
> French Dominican; **historicized Aquinas**; father of the **"signs of the times"** as *loci theologici in act* — the theological engine of [[Gaudium et Spes]]; survived pre-council repression; co-founded **Concilium**.

**Order:** Dominican · **Tendency:** progressive neo-Thomist · **Journal:** [[06 - Reception Controversies & Hermeneutics|Concilium]]

## Method
**Historicization of Aquinas** — 20th-c. theology must engage modern philosophy and the **social sciences** just as Aquinas engaged Aristotle in the 13th. Positive appreciation of history; recognized the "end of the Constantine era" (Church-State separation).

## Key concept — signs of the times
New realities (missionary expansion, pluralism, ecumenism, lay apostolate) are **theological sources — "*loci theologici* in act."** They "must enter… in the discernment of the impact of the Word in the historical community of the faithful." #signs-of-the-times

## Documents shaped
- **[[Gaudium et Spes]]** — his "signs of the times" hermeneutic is integral to the whole Pastoral Constitution (GS 44).

## Tensions & post-conciliar
Survived the pre-VII repression of the "new theology." **Founding member of Concilium** — grounding modern theology in Scripture and salvation history.
