# Gérard Philips

> [!summary] One-line
> Belgian theologian, joint secretary of the Doctrinal Commission; **chief redactor of [[Lumen Gentium]]** after the "triumphalistic" draft fell; named the Council's **"two tendencies."**

**Role:** joint secretary, Doctrinal Commission

## The "two tendencies" (1963 essay)
> *"The presence of two tendencies in the doctrinal life of the Church, the first eager to remain faithful to a more traditional message, the second more worried about the spread of the message to contemporary man, is a permanent and normal phenomenon."*

And on ressourcement: *"Transformation, it is true, does not come without pain."*

## Chief redactor of Lumen Gentium
After the initial draft on the Church was rejected as too **triumphalistic**, the Theological Commission assigned Philips to build the new draft. Result: **[[Lumen Gentium]]** — shifted from structural governance to a **"more pneumatological approach,"** emphasizing the Holy Spirit and the Church as **mystery**.
